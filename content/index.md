I'm Sebastian. 

My name is Sebastian. I work as a DevOps Engineer. I write about how I am using AI to improve my own life. 

## Hope
Humans are the most impressive and the most diappointing thing in the universe. But I tend to be impressed, not disappointed. We have gotten a long way! Everything we own today – smartphones, cars, data centres – could have been produced 20 000 years earlier: the raw materials were there, but our brains weren't up to the task yet. Imagine how the earth – or the solar system – may look like in 20 000 years from now, or even 200 years, or maybe 20 years. 

You need to have a little compassion, with yourself and the rest of the world: Life is fucking brutal, and everyone, every generation will do their best. There are monsters among men, and we should do what is necessary to stop them – but monsters are the outliars, most people just want to live their life.

## Fear
I am afraid of people who have given up, who hate the human race and believe that nothing matters. 

I am afraid of people with good intentions and horrible plans on how to achieve them. 

I am afraid of people getting easier to manipulate due to the power of images and the ever-increasing pace of life. 

I am afraid of the mass and how easy they are to manipulate. You don't want to get in the way of a running crowd, so the most important thing is to prevent panick before it is too late. 

I am afraid of people getting dumber due to AI. Bad things happened due to technology before: People got fatter and weaker when machines took over physical work. Paul Graham and Daniel Miessler write about this.
