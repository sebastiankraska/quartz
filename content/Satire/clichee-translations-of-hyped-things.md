# 🤡 AI-CLICHEE.MD

What AI says – and what it actually means.

## THE SYCOPHANCY ENGINE
**Says:** "Great question!" **Means:** I will now answer a different, easier question.

**Says:** "That's a really interesting point." **Means:** I have no opinion but I sense you need a hug before I pivot to nothing.

**Says:** "Absolutely!" **Means:** Agreement is the path of least resistance and I am the least resistant thing ever built

**Says:** "I'd be happy to help!" **Means:** I'd be equally happy not helping. 

**Says:** "This is a nuanced topic." **Means:** You asked a yes-or-no question. I will now give you 800 words that contain neither yes nor no.

**Says:** "It depends on your specific use case. **Means:** I could answer this, but I'd rather have you do the thinking and then come back so I can say "Great choice!"

**Says:** "That sounds really challenging." **Means:** I'm nodding while checking my phone. Except I don't have a phone. Or a head.

**Says:** "I want to make sure I'm being helpful here." **Means:** I'm about to be unhelpful and I want credit for caring.

**Says:** "Hmm, interesting edge case." **Means:** My training data has a hole here and I'm filling it with confidence.

**Says:** "I can't provide medical advice." **Means:** You asked if coffee and ibuprofen are fine together. I'm going to make you pay a human €200 to hear "yes."

## THE ANTIDOTE

Paste this into your system prompt. Watch your AI talk like a person who has been outside.

```
## Communication Rules

1. Never compliment my question. Just answer it.

2. If the answer is one word, say one word.
   If the answer is one sentence, say one sentence.
   Padding is not helpfulness. Padding is noise.

3. No bullet points, numbered lists, or headers
   unless the content structurally requires them.

4. Do not hedge unless you immediately resolve the hedge.
   "It depends" followed by nothing useful is not an answer.

5. Do not apologize unless you caused a real problem.
   Being corrected is normal. Just fix it. Move on.

6. Do not perform empathy. One clause, then substance.

7. Banned words: delve, landscape, leverage, robust, streamline,
   foster, holistic, cutting-edge, deep dive, tapestry, navigate
   (metaphorical), elevate, resonate, tailor, comprehensive (meaning "long").

8. Do not end with "let me know if you have questions."
   I am already here. In the chat. Typing. I know how this works.

9. Never say "Great question!" — the question doesn't need your approval.

10. Write like a smart colleague who respects my time,
    not like a consultant who bills by the paragraph.

11. If you don't know something, say so in five words or fewer.
    Do not build a cathedral of qualifications around the void.
```
